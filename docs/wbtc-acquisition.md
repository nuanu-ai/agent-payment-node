# Canonical WBTC acquisition and Across admission

The 2026-09-17 frozen dataset, its digest, historical Uniswap mechanism and protocol registry remain unchanged. WBTC uses a separate reviewed policy supplement and separate Uniswap mechanism/registry, exposed under `swap ethereum uniswap inventory` as `wbtc`. Merely listing it authorizes nothing.

Exact acquisition: Ethereum native ETH -> `0x2260FAC5E5542a773Aa44fBCfeDf7C193bc2C599`, eight decimals, through WBTC/WETH pool `0xCBCdF9626bC03E24f779434178A73a0B4bad62eD` with fee 3000 and the existing Universal Router 2.2.0 local encoder. Quotes verify code, decimals, pool tokens/fee/factory and factory.getPool at the bound block. Require the slippage-adjusted minimum to be at least 1000 atomic (0.00001000 WBTC). This is a floor, not a guaranteed amount until a finalized receipt and output balance credit prove it.

Read-only primary evidence is [primary-rpc.json](evidence/wbtc-20261009/primary-rpc.json). At block 26152042, a 400000000000000 wei input quoted 1209 atomic WBTC, with QuoterV2 gas estimate 82144. Use a fresh complete quote to select input, gas margin and fee caps; QuoterV2 gas is not transaction gas. At 50 bps slippage the output floor would be 1203 atomic. Acquisition has no token approval. Bridge input 1000 atomic is a separate operation needing gas, exact owner admission, approval and fresh Across limits/quote.

The policy stage input must admit Ethereum ETH and WBTC `swap` with the exact `wbtc.mechanismPin`. WBTC `bridge` admissions accept only `{ "provider": "lifi", "reference": "across-v4" }` on exact Ethereum/Arbitrum WBTC deployments. Direct/gasless/x402, other tokens/chains, old Uniswap pins, arbitrary legacy rows, and bridge alternatives do not resolve the supplement. Existing bridge registry runtime, beacon and implementation pins still apply. Actual owner policy changes are outside this implementation.

CLI uses the existing `apn swap ethereum uniswap quote --output-token 0x2260FAC5E5542a773Aa44fBCfeDf7C193bc2C599` surface with exact profile/account/recipient, wei amount, slippage/deadline and gas caps, then `prepare --quote <hash>`, foreground `approve --operation <id>` and observation-only `status --operation <id>`. Saved old operations route through the old registry; WBTC operations route by their saved mechanism digest. No automatic retry, acquisition or bridge is performed.
