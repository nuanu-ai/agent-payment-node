# Circle V2 EVM Fast runtime

`apn circle evm` moves Arbitrum USDC to Sei (1329), Linea (59144) or Monad (143) with CCTP V2 Fast (`depositForBurn`, `minFinalityThreshold` 1000) for any local profile. Every input is explicit and required; there is no default profile, beneficiary or amount:

`apn circle evm prepare --profile <source-profile> --destination-profile <gas-profile> --destination-chain <1329|59144|143> --idempotency-key <key> --beneficiary <address> --gross-atomic <amount> --max-issuer-fee-atomic <cap> --min-mint-atomic <floor>`

- The source signer is the custody wallet of `--profile`; it approves and burns `--gross-atomic` USDC on Arbitrum.
- The mint signer and destination gas payer is the custody wallet of `--destination-profile` (it may be the same profile). It submits `receiveMessage` and pays the destination gas.
- `--beneficiary` receives the minted USDC and need not be a local wallet. Amounts are positive uint256 atomic USDC; the issuer cap is below gross and the minimum mint is at most gross minus that cap.
- The route table (`src/circle-v2-evm/catalog.ts`) holds protocol data only: chain, CCTP domain, destination USDC, gas billing, the destination mint gas budget and the RPC variable. Adding a destination is table data.

Admission is each profile's own active policy. The source policy must bind `accounts.evm` to the source wallet and admit Arbitrum USDC and Arbitrum native on the bridge rail with mechanism `circle-cctp-v2 / circle-v2-evm-fast-42161-<destination>.v2` and caps. The destination policy must admit the destination native asset with the same mechanism up to the route's mint gas budget. An optional owner `recipients` pin on the source USDC admission (decision OD-1) must list the beneficiary; it is checked at prepare, at every pre-effect confirmation (source approval, first burn, mint) and at reservation. Without a pin the policy does not restrict beneficiaries; the beneficiary is still shown on the foreground consent screen and bound into the burn, the issuer message and the mint proof.

Use `approve-source --operation <id>`, `observe --operation <id>`, `approve-mint --operation <id>` and `status --operation <id>`. Financial commands require foreground physical TTY consent. MCP exposes review/status and an explicit CLI handoff for approval and cleanup.

The fixed v1 schema (`apn.circle-v2-evm-operation.v1`: 40100 atomic USDC to one fixed recipient) is read-only since 0.5.43: no new v1 operation is prepared, and existing v1 operations still observe, mint, clean up, retire their nonce and adopt an external mint. Records are validated against their own stored binding hash and their own custody records, never against code constants, so every journal 0.5.40-0.5.42 wrote keeps loading (`tests/fixtures/legacy-corpus/`).

Read-only v1 operations keep their `circle-v2-evm-fast-42161-<destination>.v1` reference. Shared asset usage reserves the gross USDC (40100 for v1), source approval 30000000000000 wei, source burn 30000000000000 wei, optional cleanup 15000000000000 wei and the destination native ceiling. The worst source reserve is 75000000000000 wei per route. Finalized usage conservatively counts approved approval/burn/destination caps; the unused cleanup reserve consumes 0. Receipt proofs retain the actual approval and burn fees separately. Monad actual fees use the full billed gas limit.

Signing and broadcast have separate durable marks. A crash after a mark or a lost RPC response retains holds and permits observation only; no raw transaction is resent. Frozen custody, encrypted private material, common owner locks, operation enumeration and idempotency bind both signing profiles. A canonical included burn with consumed allowance may free its source nonce domain for the next Circle route only after independent canonical recheck and latest nonce advancement; other rails retain the source hold. Budget holds and destination conflicts remain until closure.

Destination proof decodes the pinned V2 `MintAndWithdraw` event with net amount and `feeCollected`. A positive issuer fee requires exactly two zero-origin USDC transfers: the net mint to the recipient and the fee mint to the historical TokenMessenger `feeRecipient()`. The runtime reads that getter at the receipt block using EIP-1898, checks the messenger, minter and token code pins there, and rechecks the block. A zero fee requires the same V2 event and exactly one principal transfer.

Issuer signatures may authorize mint while source finality is pending. Success requires fresh independent source finalized and destination safe canonical observations, a bound nonce and zero residual allowance. The residual allowance is proven at the burn block from receipts, not read at `latest`: the confirmed approval at nonce n set the allowance to exactly the burn amount (its `Approval` event) and the burn at nonce n+1 moved that amount (its `DepositForBurn` event), so a later, unrelated approval of the same wallet cannot block the operation. Expired attestation uses read-only `refresh-attestation --operation <id>` for the same burn and nonce before any mint mark. There is no reburn or fallback.

`cleanup --operation <id>` provides explicit zero-allowance recovery after approval without a burn attempt, or after a canonical finalized source revert. A wholly unsubmitted operation can be cancelled only after checking zero allowance and absence of every encrypted effect material. Ambiguous effects cannot be cancelled. A reverted or ambiguous destination mint remains held for observation; automatic retry is unavailable.

The Circle signing domains are Arbitrum, Sei, Linea and Monad. Existing standalone Ethereum/Optimism, TRON and Solana rails have no signing-domain overlap. Sei native funding must finish before mint preparation; integration must register any native funding journal that shares the Sei owner domain.

This change supplies source, generated distribution and offline adversarial tests. It does not install the runtime, change owner policies or perform payments.

Historical v1 note — Sei also had one explicit seller gas-owner variant: `--destination-chain 1329 --destination-profile evm-live-seller`, pinned to `0x991e254B5C8e0AAf6c244eaa2706BAd059809b04`. The omitted library selector and `evm-live-buyer` preserve the historical Sei buyer route. The CLI requires the destination profile explicitly. Both variants burn buyer Arbitrum USDC and mint to the same fixed MetaMask recipient; the destination profile/custody in the immutable intent selects exactly one variant. Linea and Monad accept only their existing finite payer profiles. Seller policy must admit native Sei bridge mechanism `circle-cctp-v2 / circle-v2-evm-fast-42161-1329.v1` up to 50000000000000000 atomic native per mint; its exact active grant is frozen separately from buyer source policy.

### Expired unknown approval nonce cleanup (v1, any destination)

`apn circle evm cleanup-nonce --operation <operation-id>` is a separate foreground recovery action for an expired v1 route on any destination whose original approval is sealed and unknown, while its burn remains unsigned and unattempted. It freezes one new Arbitrum USDC `approve(spender, 0)` at the original approval nonce. The full signed gas upper must fit the existing 15000000000000 wei cleanup hold. Original approval/burn bytes, hashes, deadline and history stay unchanged.

The immutable retirement intent permanently disables the original approval and burn, including restored journals. Independent create-only SIGN/SEND claims prevent another cleanup signature or submission. Fresh private foreground authority lasts at most 60 seconds and both exact policy windows. An old-approval race, expired authority or lost RPC retains holds and permits observation only. Cleanup nonce and envelope never change after a claim.

Ordinary `circle evm observe` cannot start an unclaimed cleanup or open a foreground approval. It can observe a claimed cleanup and reconcile its finalized proof. The terminal state `nonce_retired` describes cleanup, with a separate cleanup retirement receipt; it does not claim a CCTP payment. Normal ledger reconciliation closes unused principal/source/destination holds only after the exact finalized cleanup, original nonce consumption, canonical zero allowance, intact original burn no-sign evidence and permanent tombstone. Merely included receipts or transaction absence retain all holds.

The explicit nonce cleanup captures a create-only current authority sidecar under both owner policy locks. It binds the same custody identities and parent intent to current policy digests, revisions, activation digests and their earliest window end. Historical parent policy fields and reservations remain unchanged. Current policy must re-admit retained holds; the cleanup uses the existing 15T native hold, with no duplicate reservation. Subsequent activation changes refuse further financial work, while claimed cleanup remains observation-only. The foreground code, private grant and durable SIGN/SEND claims bind this separate authority hash.

### Adopt a public external mint

`apn circle evm adopt-external-mint --operation <operation-id> --transaction-hash <canonical-mint-hash>` is an explicit public-proof settlement command for any destination of a v1 or v2 operation (CCTP V2 lets anyone relay a mint, because the burn's `destinationCaller` is zero). It cannot sign, estimate, broadcast, open financial consent or renew a policy. It requires the original verified attestation, finalized source approval and burn, zero residual allowance, and no owned mint or cleanup private entry. A caller controlled by any local APN wallet or provider profile is refused. The owned mint path continues to require its exact owner caller.

The external proof authenticates the chain-bound canonical transaction signature and header, exact receive/mint/USDC events, pinned historical deployments and attesters, safe receipt membership, and the recipient balance change at canonical parent and included block hashes. It reads the explicit transaction and exact-block token logs, with a shared 160-request, 120-second public-read budget and four concurrent requests. Create-only operation, nonce and source-message claims prevent duplicate adoption; interrupted settlement resumes idempotently. A matching, conflicting or malformed public claim blocks the owned mint before consent, estimation or custody, including after an old journal is restored.

The receipt class is `circle_external_mint_fulfillment` and the terminal journal state is `external_fulfilled`. Source principal settles normally; source native budget still counts the existing approval and burn caps (60T), while receipts show actual fees separately. Unsubmitted cleanup and destination mint holds consume zero and release only after the bound proof. Controlled destination native consumption is zero; third-party mint gas is recorded separately. Original policy snapshots, historical effects and intent remain intact even when current policies have changed or expired.

Finite V2 funding operations also support this explicit external-mint command. The proof retains the operation’s exact recipient, gross amount, issuer fee and minimum output rather than the legacy fixed amount. Adoption requires fresh FINALIZED source approval and burn, SAFE destination receipt and canonical signed transaction, the exact issuer message and attestation, pinned deployments and historical attesters, consumed nonce, zero residual allowance, and exact historical USDC credit in the canonical receipt. Later recipient spending does not invalidate that credit; adoption requires neither current balance coverage nor an archive balance query. Every owned mint and cleanup material, SIGN claim and SEND claim must be absent, including when an owned mint envelope was never created.

V2 adoption preserves the frozen purpose, profile and gas-owner identity, fingerprint, finite policies and expiry. It grants no new financial authority and needs no policy renewal or signing consent. Source principal and approval/burn caps remain conservatively USED; only the proven never-entered owned cleanup and mint holds become released_unsubmitted. The external caller’s receipt fee is reported separately and is not charged to the local destination profile. The terminal state is external_fulfilled, with a permanent operation/message/nonce tombstone that prevents a restored journal from entering owned mint or cleanup. Conflicting proofs and any prior owned SIGN/SEND entry fail closed. No historical UNKNOWN operation outside this exact purpose is released.


### Historical records (read-only)

The owner's three historical v1 operations were closed on 0.5.43: Sei `4ee24e45…` (`nonce_retired`, through the sealed-burn,
consumed-burn and cleanup85/cleanup86 recoveries), Linea `23f54a86…` (`completed`) and Monad `df077b00…`
(`external_fulfilled`). The one-off controllers and the `cleanup85-prepare`, `cleanup85-cancel` and `cleanup86-approve`
commands are deleted. Their records still load: `status`, `observe` (a terminal journal is returned as stored) and the shared
usage ledger read them through the frozen validators in `src/legacy/` (sealed/consumed-burn and cleanup85 retirement proofs,
the cleanup85 native cancellation binding and its ledger hold). The `cleanup85_recovery` sidecar is no longer projected by
`status`; the terminal journal's `nonceRetirement` proof carries the cancellation and cleanup86 evidence. `cleanup-nonce`
handles only the generic expired-unknown-approval case.

### Native Arbitrum ETH while a Circle mint completes

A direct native Arbitrum transfer from a wallet with an unresolved Circle operation normally waits for it. Once that operation's source burn is canonically FINALIZED (approval and burn confirmed, zero residual allowance), it never signs on Arbitrum again: cleanup and nonce retirement need an unburned source, and the mint is on the destination chain. A native transfer to the custody wallet of any local profile then proceeds without waiting; any other recipient still waits until the Circle operation is terminal.
