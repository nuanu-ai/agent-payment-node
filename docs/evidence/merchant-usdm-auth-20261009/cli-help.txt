Finite external USDm merchant transfer-proof payment and delivery.

Usage:
  apn x402 merchant <command> [options]

Subgroups:
  (none)

Commands:
  apn x402 merchant prepare --profile <profile> --max-native-fee-wei <wei> --idempotency-key <key> — Freeze the exact x402engine Bitcoin-price USDm transfer-proof request on MegaETH.
  apn x402 merchant approve --operation <operation-id> — Foreground-confirm, sign and submit the frozen USDm transfer once, then deliver its proof after finalized receipt.
  apn x402 merchant observe --operation <operation-id> [--deliver] — Observe an attempted USDm payment; explicitly deliver or retry the same proof with --deliver.
  apn x402 merchant status --operation <operation-id> — Read local merchant payment and delivery evidence separately.

Machine contract: apn help --json
Detailed help: apn help x402 merchant <child>
